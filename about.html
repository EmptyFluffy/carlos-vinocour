A little context

Architecture is my starting point.

I’m Carlos, an architect and computational designer based in Costa Rica, with ten years across design, documentation and construction.

I have worked on the architect-of-record team for houses by Zaha Hadid Architects and Antoine Predock at Peninsula Papagayo, on competitions and built work at Gensler New York, and as design lead in my own studio. I lead coordination between design architects, consultants and site, and I write the Grasshopper and Dynamo tools that carry complex geometry into Revit and construction documents.

Alongside architecture I build software: Prizma, a live model of an architecture practice, and PivotHop, a platform that reads live job postings.
