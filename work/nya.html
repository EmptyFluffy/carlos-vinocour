NYA / a growing town

A mixed-use community considered as a phased and evolving urban landscape.

Place

Context

Contribution

A closer look

NYA brings together housing, services, retail and a town center. The proposal explores a tropical architectural language alongside a phased approach to development.

The selected imagery moves between the masterplan, landscape and pedestrian scale. It records an interest in how an urban framework can accommodate change over time.
