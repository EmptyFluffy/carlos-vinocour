La Perla / reading the land first

A condominium on 5.7 hectares at Matapalo, planned from the ground: its height, its slope and the paths of the rain were read before a single lot was drawn.

Place
Matapalo, Guanacaste, Costa Rica
Context
Studio CAVA · master plan study, 2021
Contribution
Site analysis and master plan, with Melissa Araya

Eight readings, one plan.

The land was modelled from the topographic survey and read in layers: contours lifted into three dimensions, elevation, slope and runoff. Each reading answers a practical question. Gentle ground is where a road or a house costs least to build, the steepest is left as it is, and where the rain gathers the water has to be led away and kept clear of buildings.

The road follows the gentlest ground in a single loop that ends in a turning circle. The houses sit in rows along one side and in a fan around the circle, and each was checked against the paths of the water before the plan was fixed.

Analysis in Rhino and Grasshopper. The film was drawn frame by frame in code from the same layers: plan and axonometric of each reading, cut together on a beat.

The readings

The plan.

33 houses in four types, 6,510 m², and four shared buildings, 610 m²: a clubhouse inside the loop, a lookout on the high edge, a bathhouse and a garden kitchen.
