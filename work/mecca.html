Jabal Omar / a hotel facing the Haram

An invited competition for a Four Seasons hotel and two residential towers beside the Grand Mosque. Jabal Omar Development invited four firms, Foster + Partners among them.

Place
Mecca, Saudi Arabia
Context
Invited competition · Gensler · 2016
Contribution
Concept designer: the Grasshopper scripts that measured the view from every room to the Haram and set each opening and balcony to it, the documentation and the competition presentations

A closer look

The scheme sits between the city and the Grand Mosque, on a site that steps down over several levels.

I worked on the concept and wrote the Grasshopper scripts that tuned every opening and balcony to the view of the Haram, then documented the scheme and built the competition presentations.

Views as a design input.

One script scored every room by what it could see of the Haram; a second set the openings and balconies from that score, tower by tower.

The massing was worked out in ten steps, from the platform over the levels of the site to the towers set on the line to the Kaaba, each step checked against the view.
