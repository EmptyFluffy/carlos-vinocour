Mall of the Emirates / a facade as a field

An ideas competition to give the Mall of the Emirates a new face: a field of nickel-plated tubes that shimmers as you move past it, opened by screens wherever the programme pushes out.

Place
Dubai, UAE
Context
Ideas competition · Gensler · 2018
Contribution
Concept designer: the facade and its geometry in Rhino and Grasshopper, the sections and elevations, the glazed connection to the existing mall, the competition presentation

A closer look

In plan the building pinches at the centre, so the sheen of the nickel changes with every viewpoint. The screens are LED glass: their colour and motion follow the media inside, the activity on each floor and the weather.

The skin: tubes, light and screens

The skin never reads the same twice: the nickel answers the sun and the viewer, and the screens set into it change with the programme.

Elevation and section

I developed the facade and its geometry in Rhino and Grasshopper, drew the sections and the elevations, and modelled the glazed connection to the existing mall.
