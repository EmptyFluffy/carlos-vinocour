A closer look

The proposal uses precast frames as the basis of a modular housing system. Transport constraints, structural logic and the arrangement of openings become inputs to the design.

Assembly

Transport and structure

The portfolio follows the proposal from individual frames to inhabitable units and a collective site arrangement. Assembly speed is a design ambition of the study, rather than a measured construction result.
