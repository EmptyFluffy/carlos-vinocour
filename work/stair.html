Capital Group / an elliptical stair

An elliptical stair through three floors of a Capital Group office in New York, from sketch to steel. Built.

Place
New York City, USA
Context
Gensler New York · built, 2018
Contribution
Job captain for the stair: its complete construction document set, the parametric script that set out the elliptical run, and coordination with the consultants and the fabricator through construction

A closer look

The stair is the one move in a quiet, three-storey workplace: a single elliptical run, wrapped in a brass guardrail, that ties the floors together around the lobby.

I produced the complete construction document set for the stair, wrote the parametric script that set out its elliptical run, and coordinated with the consultants and the fabricator through construction.

The stair in plan

The stair stands in the same place on every floor, beside the reception and the cafe, so each floor reads as a landing.

From sketch to steel
