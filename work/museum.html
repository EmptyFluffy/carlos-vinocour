Military museum / a roof of sails

A competition entry for a military museum: a run of tensile canopies along the edge of a headland, above the sea.

Place
An island in Italy
Context
Competition entry · 2025
Contribution
Concept and form-finding, with Joshua Chaves: the Grasshopper script, with Kangaroo, that relaxed the cloth between the masts until each canopy curved two ways at once

A roof that began with sails.

The roof began with sails: light cloth held in tension, curving two ways at once. A run of canopies follows the edge of the headland, and masts carry the cloth over the stone terraces and the path along the cliff.

Under the canopies the museum stays open to the sea air and the view, in shade through the day and lit from below at night.

The canopy, form-found

The cloth was relaxed in Kangaroo between the masts until each canopy curved two ways at once, like a sail.

Inspiration

Competition entry by Carlos Vinocour and Joshua Chaves. Visualization: Emanuel Baizán. Sail photographs: Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
