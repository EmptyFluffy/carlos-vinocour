Papagayo 404 / a house extension

An extension of about 1,100 m² to a house raised on slanted steel columns above the dry forest.

Place
Península Papagayo, Costa Rica
Context
Studio CAVA · built 2020
Contribution
Project architect and design lead; drawings signed with Melissa Araya

A closer look

Papagayo 404 is a house on slanted steel columns over the dry forest of the Papagayo Peninsula, above the bay. Studio CAVA designed its extension: the entrance sequence, the kitchen and living room, two bathrooms, the pools and the jacuzzi, the decks, a sunken terrace with a fire pit and an outdoor kitchen.

The sunken terrace

A long infinity pool runs along the edge of the lot. Timber decks tie the water to the kitchen and the double-height living room, and at the end of the terrace rings of built-in seating step down to a square fire pit under a canopy on two raked posts. The drawings come from the construction set, annotated in Spanish as they were drawn for the builders.
