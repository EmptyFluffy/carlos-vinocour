Pavilion / form-finding

An exploration of concrete, flexible formwork and the relationship between a repeating part and a larger field.

Design and material exploration

A closer look

A semi-elastic formwork combines fabric faces with rigid edges. The edges establish connections between modules, while the fabric allows the concrete to take a less predetermined form.

From experiment to module

The study moves between material experiments, morphological references and a layered pavilion geometry. The portfolio presents both the construction logic of the module and the spatial possibilities of repetition.
