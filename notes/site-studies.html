Working idea · October 2026

Site Studies

What if the first design move was a clearer understanding of the ground?

I’m exploring the idea of a modular Grasshopper toolkit that brings terrain, trees and planning constraints into a shared site model. The aim is to compare possible locations for a building and make the reasoning visible.

Terrain + treesConstraintsSuitable areasAlternatives

Several components, one conversation

Each component would remain useful on its own: reading terrain, mapping slopes, protecting trees, defining constraints or comparing footprints. The architect could change priorities and inspect the consequences.

  • Separate mandatory constraints from design preferences.
  • Compare alternatives for earthworks, tree preservation and access.
  • Show the source and assumptions behind each input.

This is a project concept, not a released plugin. The first experiment would use a single site with verified inputs and a limited family of footprints.

It continues a thread from the Tamarindo water and urbanism study ↗.
